Paso 3 · Producción libre (constraint writing)

Escribe 2-3 frases describiendo a un/a amigo/a. Usa al menos 3 de estos adjetivos: cheerful, confident, generous, honest, loyal.

have to / don't have to

Afirmativa
I / You / We / They have to study.
He / She / It has to study.
Negativa
I / You / We / They don't have to study.
He / She / It doesn't have to study.
Preguntas
Do I/you/we/they have to study? · Does he/she/it have to study?
